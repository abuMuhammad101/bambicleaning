Still unsure? We’re a call or a message away.

How is my price calculated?

Your total depends on the size of your home (bedrooms and bathrooms), the type of clean, how often you’d like us to come, and any add-ons you choose. You see the full price before you book.

How do I pay?

A 20% deposit confirms your booking. It’s paid securely online when you finalize, and the balance is due after your clean.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled clean to avoid a $75 cancellation fee. You can track and manage your booking with your Booking ID.

Do I need to be home?

No. When you book, tell us how we’ll get in: someone at home, a door code, a lockbox, or a key left for us. Please secure valuables before your visit.

Are you pet-friendly?

Absolutely. Let us know about your dog or cat (and their name!) when you book, along with any special instructions.

Do you clean offices and new builds?

Yes. Commercial and post-construction cleans are quoted individually. Call us at (734) 360-6176 or request a quote and we’ll follow up with a custom proposal.
