# Homepage references: Contra × Programa → Bambi

Source: Mobbin, sites category (studied 2026-10-08). Links go to the Mobbin captures.
**Contra** sets the base aesthetic. **Programa** supplies selected layout patterns and components.

## 1. Contra: the base aesthetic

### What makes it feel like Contra
| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Type pairing** | A tight, heavy grotesk for the main statement, set against a **light, high-contrast serif for one or two words in the same line** (e.g. "How to hire *on Contra*", "Frequently asked / *questions*", "Why clients *trust Contra*"). This sans/serif mix within one headline is the signature move. |
| **Headline scale** | Very large hero headlines (about 64–88px), tight leading (about 1.0–1.05) and tight tracking. Sub-copy is short, grey and two lines at most. |
| **Canvas** | Mostly white or near-white, with whole sections switching to **near-black (#1A1A1E-ish)** or a **flat soft lavender** band for contrast. |
| **Glassy 3D objects** | Iridescent, pearlescent 3D shapes (stars, rings, ribbons) float at the edges of the hero and section corners. They're decoration only and never sit behind text. |
| **Accent** | One saturated accent (Contra violet `#6155F5`-ish) used **only** for the primary CTA and the odd gradient word ("*Scale* without limits"). Everything else is black, white and grey. |
| **Buttons** | Fully rounded pills. Primary is solid dark (or the accent); secondary is a 1px outline pill. Small text (14–15px, medium weight). |
| **Cards** | Large 16–20px radius, white on tinted backgrounds, very soft shadows, generous padding. Testimonial cards put the quote first and the avatar, name and role at the bottom. |
| **Chips/tags** | Small outlined pills used for filters, suggested searches and skill tags. |
| **Rhythm** | Lots of air (120–160px between sections). Each section has one idea, a headline on the left, and prev/next arrow buttons on the right when there's a carousel. |

## 3. The blend for Bambi

### Visual language
- **Base = Contra:**
  - white canvas;
  - big grotesk headlines with **one italic serif word** per heading;
  - pill buttons and outlined chips;
  - generous spacing;
  - one dark band and one tinted band per page.
- **Bambi's own palette instead of violet:**
  - deep **sage `#606C5A`** takes the role of Contra's near-black (dark bands, dark pills);
  - **sand `#DFBF90`** takes the role of Contra's violet accent (primary CTA);
  - **off-white `#FFFDFA`** for the canvas;
  - pale **sage tint** (`#EEF0EA`-ish) replaces Contra's lavender band.
- **From Programa:**
  - real photography of homes;
  - small product-moment UI cards that show the booking working;
  - stat tiles;
  - the photo-led closing CTA.
- **Decor:** swap Contra's glass 3D shapes for **soft soap-bubble / pearlescent spheres** (same iridescent feel, and it fits cleaning). Use them sparingly at the hero edges and the closing CTA.
- **Type:**
  - keep a grotesk for headings (or retire Japandi for something like Inter Tight or Geist);
  - add a light serif (e.g. Instrument Serif or Fraunces Light) for the accent words;
  - body copy in the same grotesk.

### Things deliberately **not** carried over
- Contra's violet and purple gradients. They're off-brand for a cleaning company.
- Programa's highlighter yellow. Bambi's sand plays the "one loud CTA colour" role.
- Programa's photo-free product UI as the hero. Bambi's hero leads with the quote bar.
